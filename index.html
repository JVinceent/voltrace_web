About Voltrace

Built to answer one question: where is the power going?

We help electric cooperatives find system loss faster. Our monitors read every transformer that matters, and our software turns those readings into a short list of where crews should inspect first, so honest customers stop paying for power they never used.

A Philippines where every kilowatt-hour can be traced, and system-loss charges no longer appear on anyone's electricity bill.

It started with a store owner who barely used her appliances but paid over ₱1,000 a month. She asked us, "Hindi ko naman ginagamit, bakit kailangan ko bayaran?" Her question pointed to a bigger one: where is the power going? Voltrace is our answer.
